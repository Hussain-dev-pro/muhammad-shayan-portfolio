Problem, solution and result

Problem

The distributor used spreadsheets for customer invoices, supplier bills and bank payments. When customers paid several invoices in one transfer, the owner couldn't reliably tell which invoices were still open.

Solution

Configured sales, purchase and bank journals in Odoo. Loaded opening balances and unpaid invoices and bills as of an agreed date. Tested a partial customer payment, a payment covering multiple invoices and a supplier credit.

Result

The owner can see outstanding receivables and payables in Odoo, and the opening trial balance agrees to the source records.

What was going wrong

  • Invoices, bills and bank payments lived in three separate spreadsheets.
  • Customers paid several invoices in one transfer, or paid only part of one, with no link back to the invoices.
  • The owner could not say which invoices were open, what each customer owed, or which bills were due after credits.

Results

Highlights from the engagement

  • Sales, purchase, bank and miscellaneous journals configured in Odoo 17 Enterprise.
  • Every unpaid invoice and bill loaded as its own open document at the agreed cut-over date.
  • An opening-balance clearing account used to prove nothing was counted twice.
  • Tests: partial customer payment, one payment for several invoices, supplier credit.
  • Opening trial balance agreed to the source records; ageing reports handed over to the owner.

Executive summary

The client is an early-stage wholesale distributor in the United States that buys finished goods from suppliers and sells them on credit terms to business customers. Until this project it ran its finances on spreadsheets: one for customer invoices, one for supplier bills and a third for bank payments in and out.

The breaking point was customer remittances. Customers regularly paid several invoices with a single bank transfer, and sometimes paid only part of an invoice. With no link between a payment and the invoices it settled, the owner could not reliably say which invoices were still open, how much each customer really owed, or which supplier bills were still due after credits.

The work moved the accounting into Odoo 17 Enterprise in four parts: configuration of the company, chart of accounts, payment terms, partners and core journals; opening balances loaded at an agreed cut-over date, with every unpaid invoice and bill loaded as its own open document; tests of the three scenarios that caused the most trouble; and verification and handover of the receivable and payable reports.
