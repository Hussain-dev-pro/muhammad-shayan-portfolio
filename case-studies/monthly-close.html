Problem, solution and result

Problem

Invoices and bills were recorded in Odoo, but missing receipts, unmatched customer payments and unreconciled bank and credit card transactions delayed the close. The owner couldn't see what customers owed or which bills were due.

Solution

Set up a monthly close checklist in Odoo. Collected missing records, posted outstanding bills, matched customer payments, reconciled bank and card balances to statements, reviewed overdue invoices and upcoming payments, and flagged items for the owner.

Result

The owner receives a monthly P&L, balance sheet and a short follow-up list. Cash balances tie to the statements, and the ageing reports show which invoices to chase and which bills to pay.

What was going wrong

  • Card receipts and some supplier bills never reached the books.
  • Customer payments sat unmatched, so paid invoices still looked unpaid.
  • Bank and card lines were left unreconciled, and monthly reports arrived late or not at all.

Highlights from the engagement

  • Monthly close checklist built in the Odoo Project app as a recurring task with sub-tasks.
  • Missing bills and receipts collected; outstanding bills posted.
  • Customer payments matched to invoices; bank and credit card reconciled to statements.
  • Overdue invoices and upcoming bills reviewed; owner questions tracked in one place.
  • Monthly owner pack: profit and loss, balance sheet and a short follow-up list.

Executive summary

The client is a small US business that invoices customers and buys from suppliers on credit. The team already recorded invoices and bills in Odoo during the month, so the problem was not a lack of data entry. The problem was that the month never properly closed.

Three kinds of loose ends kept the books from being finished: receipts for card and bank purchases and some supplier bills had not reached the books; money had arrived in the bank but was not matched to the invoices it paid; and statement lines sat unreconciled, so cash in Odoo could not be agreed to the bank and card statements.

The solution is a monthly close checklist built in the Odoo Project app with a fixed sequence of steps, from collecting missing records to an owner pack. Each month the owner receives a profit and loss statement, a balance sheet and a short list of items to follow up on.
