Problem, solution and result

Problem

Open carrier bills, warehouse invoices and supplier credits were tied to duplicate or inactive records. At cutover, finance needed to know which balances were outstanding and which supplier each payment belonged to.

Solution

Exported the QuickBooks Desktop trial balance, ageing reports and open transactions. Merged duplicate contacts, mapped the chart of accounts, imported unpaid invoices, bills and credits under the correct contacts, loaded remaining balances and reconciled to the exports.

Result

Finance pays pre-cutover bills, applies existing credits and collects outstanding invoices in Odoo. Opening balances tie back to QuickBooks, and older transactions stay available for reference.

What was going wrong

  • The same carrier existed under two or three names, with bills and credits split between them.
  • Inactive records still held open bills and credits.
  • Customers were split across several records, putting payments, credits and collections at risk.

Highlights from the engagement

  • QuickBooks Desktop reports and open transactions exported, including inactive names.
  • Duplicate and inactive contacts merged through a signed-off merge map.
  • Open carrier bills, warehouse invoices and supplier credits imported under the correct contacts.
  • Opening balances loaded and reconciled to the QuickBooks exports per master contact.
  • QuickBooks history archived in the Odoo Documents app for reference.

Executive summary

The client is a US logistics business providing freight and warehousing services: it buys transport from carriers and invoices customers for freight, storage and handling. Its accounting ran on QuickBooks Desktop for several years and was moved to Odoo 17 Enterprise.

Years of use had left the contact lists untidy. The same carrier existed under two or three names, old records had been made inactive while bills or credits were still open on them, and some customers were split across several records. At the switch, open carrier bills could be paid twice or not at all, and supplier credits could be forgotten.

The migration exported the reports and open items, cleaned and merged duplicate contacts through a signed-off merge map, mapped the chart of accounts, imported invoices, bills and credits under the correct master contacts, loaded and reconciled the remaining balances, and archived the QuickBooks history in Odoo for reference.
