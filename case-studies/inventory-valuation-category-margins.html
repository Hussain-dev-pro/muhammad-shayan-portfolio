Problem, solution and result

Problem

Product categories used inconsistent cost accounts and returns were recorded without the matching cost adjustment, so gross margin looked too high in one category and too low in another.

Solution

Configured Odoo's product-category mappings for the retail model, reviewed inventory and COGS accounts, traced purchases, deliveries and returns through the ledger, reconciled valuation to the general ledger and built a margin-by-category report.

Result

Finance can compare margins across electronics, home goods and beauty, trace unusual changes to specific transactions, and reconcile month-end inventory valuation to the general ledger.

What was going wrong

  • Product categories pointed to mixed or shared cost and inventory accounts, so cost landed in the wrong place.
  • Returns were recorded as credit notes only, reversing revenue but not the cost of goods.
  • Category margins moved for accounting reasons, not real profitability.

Highlights from the engagement

  • Each product category (Electronics, Home Goods, Beauty) mapped to its own income, COGS and inventory accounts.
  • Automated valuation and one costing method across all stocked categories.
  • Purchases, deliveries, customer returns and vendor returns traced through the ledger.
  • Month-end valuation reconciled to the general ledger, category by category.
  • Gross margin by category report built on the ledger, with drill-down to journal items.

Executive summary

The client is a retailer selling three product families: Electronics, Home Goods and Beauty. Finance reviews gross margin by category every month to decide on pricing, purchasing and promotions, and those decisions are only as good as the margin figures behind them.

Two accounting issues were distorting the numbers. Categories pointed to different or shared cost of goods sold and inventory accounts without a clear design, so one category's margin looked too high while another carried cost that was not its own. And customer returns were recorded only as credit notes, reversing revenue but not the cost of the returned goods.

The project configured Odoo's product-category mappings so each category has its own income, COGS and valuation accounts and the same valuation rules; traced sample purchases, deliveries and returns through the ledger; reconciled month-end inventory valuation to the general ledger category by category; and built a gross margin by category report with drill-down to the journal items behind every figure.
